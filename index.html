    How this model was made

    Project locations come from street addresses run through the US Census geocoder; heights, unit counts and status come from City of San Rafael project pages, Marin County, CEQA filings and SF YIMBY as of October 2026. Footprints are sized from unit counts and are approximate. Years after 2026 are reported targets where a developer or agency gave one, and model assumptions otherwise; each card says which.

    Terrain, shoreline, freeways and rail are hand-traced from geocoded points and USGS summit elevations, with hills exaggerated 1.6×. Existing buildings are generated to match each neighborhood's density, not drawn parcel by parcel.

    Flood zones switch on when the scenario's water level passes the exposure points reported in Marin's BayWAVE assessment, San Rafael's 2020 Flood Risk & SLR Adaptation Report and TAM's 2024 vulnerability memo. The Canal and Spinnaker zones add about 0.4 in a year of measured ground sinking. These are approximations for exploring, not engineering flood maps.

    Illustrative growth after 2034 fills sites the 2023 Housing Element, Downtown Precise Plan and General Plan 2040 identify for housing, at roughly the pace General Plan 2040 projects. No developer has proposed these buildings.
